# Travel and expense policy

## Airfare

Employees must book economy class for flights under six hours. Premium economy for longer flights requires written approval before booking.

## Hotel rates

The hotel reimbursement cap is USD 180 per night before taxes. An exception requires written approval from Finance before booking.

## Expense submission

Submit expense claims within 30 calendar days of returning from a business trip. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over USD 25.
