# Information security policy

## Incident reporting

Suspected information security incidents must be reported to the security operations team within one hour of discovery. Include the time of discovery and the affected system.

## Multifactor authentication

Multifactor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and administrative accounts. Lost authentication devices must be reported immediately.

## Removable media

Company data may be copied to removable storage only when the device is encrypted and approved by Information Security.
