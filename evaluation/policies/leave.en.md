# Employee leave policy

## Annual leave requests

Requests for five or more consecutive working days of annual leave must be submitted at least 15 calendar days before the leave starts.

## Sick leave documents

A medical certificate is required when sick leave exceeds two consecutive working days. Attach the certificate in the HR portal.

## Carryover

Employees may carry forward up to five unused annual leave days. Carried days expire on March 31 of the following year.
